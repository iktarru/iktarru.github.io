Privacy policy

Perederzhka Backup is used only by its owner.

  • It requests a single Google permission, drive.file: access only to files this tool itself creates. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Google Drive.
  • It uploads backup files that are encrypted before they leave the server. Google receives only ciphertext; the decryption key is kept by the owner outside Google.
  • It does not read Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data, and shares nothing with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/iktarru/iktarru.github.io.
